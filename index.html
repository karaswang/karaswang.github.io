About

Hi, I'm Kara Wang.
Welcome to My Space

I'm a high school senior with a strong interest in chemistry and biology, and I hope to pursue the pre-med track in college. I'm passionate about people care, volunteering, and community service. Outside the classroom, I'm a violinist and pianist with numerous competition awards, and I regularly play piano and violin at senior homes to bring music and companionship to residents. I also founded LetterLink, a pen-pal program connecting people of all ages with the companionship and consistent attention they deserve. This site brings together my research, music, and community work. Feel free to explore, get involved in my projects, or reach out and connect.
